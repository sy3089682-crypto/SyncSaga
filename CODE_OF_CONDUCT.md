# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want SyncSaga to be a technical project where people can collaborate without harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

Contributors should:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, decisions, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good intent while investigating technical problems.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy and never publish private credentials or personal information.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, malicious disclosure of private information, and intentionally disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Include enough context to investigate the incident without unnecessarily sharing private information.
